### 💜 About me

I'm a developer in transition from banking operations to software. For 3+ years at **IBM Consulting** I've worked on BPO projects for major Brazilian banks — and today I'm on the **Quality & Transformation** team, where I build **AI agents and automations** that make real operations faster.

- 🤖 Built AI agents with **Microsoft Copilot Studio** for document analysis and process validation, contributing to a **70% reduction in contract processing time**
- 💻 Building web and mobile apps with **React, TypeScript and Supabase**
- 🎓 Computer Science at FAM — graduating in 2027
- 🔐 Interested in **Cybersecurity** and **Mainframe (z/OS) Security**
- 🌱 Currently learning: Node.js, testing with Jest, and ethical hacking fundamentals

I bring something most junior devs don't have yet: years inside the business processes that software is supposed to improve.
